RESEARCH EXESTING SYSTEMS

1. Real-World Systems

Purdue Course Signals: gives each student a red, yellow or green risk level based on grades and online activity.

OU Analyse (Open University, UK): predicts each week which students may fail and alerts tutors.

2. Common Approaches

Models: Decision Tree, Random Forest, Logistic/Linear Regression, SVM, Neural Networks.

Key features: past grades, attendance, failures, study time.

Problems:
Models rely heavily on past grades, so warnings come late.
Pass/fail data is often imbalanced.
Datasets are often small.

3. This Project

Predict final grades early, without relying only on past grades.

Compare several models using proper metrics (F1, MAE, RMSE).

Show the most important factors that affect performance.
